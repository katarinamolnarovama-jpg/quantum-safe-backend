## STEP 4: Tell Render to use Docker

1. Go to **https://dashboard.render.com**
2. Click on **quantum-safe-api**
3. Click **"Settings"** (left sidebar)
4. Scroll down to find **"Runtime"** or **"Language"**
5. Change it from **Python** to **Docker**
6. For the **Dockerfile Path** field type: `./Dockerfile`
7. **Delete** the Build Command field (clear it completely)
8. Click **"Save Changes"**

---

## STEP 5: Deploy

1. Click **"Manual Deploy"** (top right)
2. Click **"Deploy latest commit"**
3. Wait **5-10 minutes** (Docker builds take longer)
4. Watch the logs — you should see:
```
Installing liboqs-dev...
✅ liboqs installed
Successfully installed liboqs-python
```
